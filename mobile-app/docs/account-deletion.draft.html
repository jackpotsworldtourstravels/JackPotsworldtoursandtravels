Delete your JackpotsWorld account

JackpotsWorld Tours & Travel · Hyderabad, India

You can ask us to delete your JackpotsWorld account and the personal data we hold about you. This page explains how to ask, what happens next, and what we can and cannot delete.

What happens when you ask

Asking is a request. It does not delete your account straight away. A member of our team has to review it, check that it really comes from you, and complete it. Until then your account stays as it is and keeps working. We respond within 30 days of verifying your identity.

How to ask

In the app. Open the JackpotsWorld app, go to Profile, and choose Delete my account. Read the explanation, tick the box to confirm you understand, and send the request. If you are using the app as a guest (without an account), give us an email address or mobile number we can reach you on, because we have no other way to reply.

Without the app. Write to support@jackpotsworldtours.com from the email address on your account or booking, or call +91 91778 47799. Say that you want your account and personal data deleted.

Either way, we will ask you to verify your identity before we act. A request to delete an account is exactly the kind of thing an impersonator might send, so we check it with you first, as our Privacy Policy describes.

What we can delete

  • Your profile: name, contact details, date of birth, gender and address.
  • Saved travellers, including passport details, once no trip or claim needs them.
  • Your wishlist and saved preferences.
  • Your sign-in on your devices.

What we must keep

The law, not our preference, decides some of this. As our Privacy Policy explains:

  • Booking records, invoices and payment references are kept for as long as tax, accounting and company law require, which in India is generally at least eight financial years from the end of the relevant year.
  • Travel documents for a trip that has not happened yet, or that has an open refund, claim or dispute, are kept until it is closed, then deleted unless a statutory record requires otherwise.
  • Correspondence, including your deletion request, is kept for up to three years from the last contact, so that a later question about an old trip can still be answered.

Anything we keep is used only for the reason we are keeping it.

If you have a booking coming up

You can still send your request. But records for a trip that has not happened yet are kept until it is over, so we cannot complete your request until then. If you have an unpaid booking you no longer want, you can cancel it from its page in the app first. If you want to cancel a paid booking, ask us and our team will review it.

What you will hear from us

We will contact you on the email address or mobile number on your account (or the one you gave for a guest request) to verify your identity, tell you what we have deleted and what we have kept and why, and confirm when it is done. If we cannot complete part of your request, we will say which part and why.

Questions or complaints

Write to support@jackpotsworldtours.com or call +91 91778 47799. Our Privacy Policy explains your other rights and how to complain if you are not satisfied with our response.
